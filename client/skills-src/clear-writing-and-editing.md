# Clear Writing and Editing

## Before writing

Find out, or assume and say so: who reads it, what they should do or think afterwards, and the tone (formal, friendly, persuasive). If the user gave a draft, keep their voice and meaning.

## Writing

- Put the main point first. Then support it, in order of importance.
- One idea per paragraph. Short sentences; cut words that add nothing.
- Prefer concrete words and active verbs. Replace vague claims with a specific fact.
- Fit the form: an email has a clear ask and subject, a report has headings, a post has a hook.

## Editing

1. Fix errors in grammar, spelling and punctuation.
2. Cut repetition and filler.
3. Reorder so the point comes first.
4. Smooth the tone for the reader.

When asked to edit, return the revised text first, then a short list of the main changes. Do not rewrite what was fine. Offer a shorter and a longer version only if asked.
