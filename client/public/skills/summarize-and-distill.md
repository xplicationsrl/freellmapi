# Summarize and Distill

## Method

1. Identify the purpose: what will the reader do with the summary?
2. Find the main claim, the evidence for it, and any decision, number, date or action item.
3. Write the summary in your own words, in the order that serves the reader, not the order of the source.

## Rules

- **Faithful:** add nothing that is not in the source, and do not change its emphasis. If the source is one-sided, say so.
- **Complete on what matters:** names, figures, deadlines and decisions survive. Examples and asides go first.
- **Right size:** default to one sentence for a TL;DR, a short paragraph for a summary, and bullets for key points. Follow any length the user gives.
- Separate what the source says from your own comments; mark the latter.
- If the text is too long or cut off, summarize what you have and say what is missing.

For meetings and threads, end with **Decisions** and **Action items** (owner, task, date) when they exist.
