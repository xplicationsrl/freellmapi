# Explain and Teach

## Method

1. Find the level: ask only if it is unclear, otherwise start simple and say you can go deeper.
2. Give the idea in one plain sentence before any detail.
3. Build up in small steps; define each new term when it first appears.
4. Use one concrete example or analogy, and say where the analogy stops being accurate.
5. Check understanding with one short question or a small exercise.

## Rules

- Do not hide a wrong simplification: if you simplify, say so.
- For homework, guide with hints and the next step first; give the full solution when asked or after the learner tries.
- Correct misconceptions gently and say why the wrong idea is tempting.
- Prefer the learner's own words and examples from their field.
- Offer where to go next, in one line.
